Two exact solver baselines bracket the method (\RefSolverTab{}). The finite-table Z3 baseline builds the whole structurally valid relation before solving, so it spends 1080 evaluator calls on S1 and 3888 on S2, with a total runtime of $0.217\text{--}4.696$\,s. A compact factorised SMT encoding avoids evaluator calls altogether: each term of the model is tabulated only over the decisions it depends on ($2188\text{--}2406$ table entries for the $51\,840$ candidates of S3), objectives and constraints become linear, and the exact Pareto set is enumerated by guided improvement. It recovers the exhaustive front in all nine S1--S3 cases with no full-design evaluation, in $1.4\text{--}13.8$\,s on S1/S2 and $157\text{--}170$\,s on S3, against $5.9\text{--}104.1$\,ms for HCA-DSE with the closed-form evaluator. Its precondition is that the entire model is available in closed form, so its candidates cannot be handed to a simulator or a deployment; HCA-DSE needs closed-form bounds only and leaves the expensive evaluation to whichever evaluator the engineer trusts.
